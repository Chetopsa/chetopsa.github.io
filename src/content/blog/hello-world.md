I've been meaning to add a blog here for a while. Mostly I want somewhere to write up the
things I build — the parts that don't fit in a README, and the problems that took a lot
longer to solve than they should have.

## What goes here

Probably a mix of:

- Notes on projects I'm working on, and what I'd do differently next time
- Things I learned the hard way about systems programming
- The occasional run, since I apparently track those publicly now

No schedule. Posts land when they're done.
